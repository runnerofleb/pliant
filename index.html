2 · Teach what an RA actually is

Educate

"The Registered Agent is the official legal point of contact for state and legal notices for your LLC. It's a legal requirement, and whoever is listed needs to have a physical address in the same state where the business is registered."

"The important part: whoever is listed is responsible for physically receiving official documents for the business. So if someone needs to deliver a state notice, lawsuit, compliance notice, or other legal docs — they show up at that address, often unannounced, during business hours."

"If you aren't there, the document may not get received — and the consequences for missing legal docs can include lost liability protection and default judgments. It can be pretty serious."

"So if you list yourself, you're personally responsible for making sure those documents are received and handled. If something important gets missed, it creates compliance issues and legal headaches — for you."

⚖️ 1 · Tax classification election

"At a high level, the very first thing to consider is notifying the IRS how you want [Business] taxed — your tax classification election: C-Corp, S-Corp, accept the IRS default, or change between partnership and disregarded-entity status. Once your EIN is issued, if you don't proactively choose, the IRS assigns a default — which may or may not be in your best interest, because there's no one-size-fits-all in taxes."

  • To change: Form 8832 and/or Form 2553 — the IRS requires receipt within 75 days of when you want it effective
  • "Your classification determines how you'll be paying yourself — not a choice to leave to the IRS, right [Name]? You might ask a tax expert: 'what election would lower my self-employment taxes?'"
  • Headline: "This decision impacts what happens with your money — it should be tailored to your situation, and speaking with a tax professional is highly recommended."
  • ZB files the S-Corp election (Form 2553) free within the first 30 days after your formation purchase; $200 after 30 days; after 75 days it's a late filing — still nothing beyond the $200
Tie-down

"How confident do you feel about making the right decision on this?" / "Do you think this is something to talk to a tax professional about before deciding?" / "Do you see why this is important to discuss before the decision is defaulted for you by the IRS?"

📚
Only if they ask for detail: Single-member default = disregarded entity (income/expenses on Form 1040, Schedule C). Multi-member default = partnership (Form 1065 + Schedule K-1 to each member, reported on personal returns). C-Corp = corporate income tax rates, elected via Form 8832 within 75 days. S-Corp = pass-through of profits/losses/deductions/credits avoiding double taxation, via Form 2553 — requires ≤100 shareholders, all U.S. citizens or residents.

📆 2 · Quarterly estimated taxes

Hook

"I agree — now let's talk about something that catches almost every LLC owner off guard… Unlike a regular job, the IRS expects you to pay taxes on your business profit every few months — not just at annual tax time.   • Expect to owe $1,000+ for the year? You pay as you go — usually four installments: Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15
  • Miss or underpay → penalties and interest, even if you're profitable and file on time annually
